## Visual hierarchy and geometry

- Brand remains crisp diamond PROJECT / gold DOLLAR / emerald money bag. In the target it occupies the upper 15–20% of the panel, with its single research subtitle directly underneath. Keep the existing high-resolution 1800 × 693 brand source, not a new redraw.
- The entrance dominates nearly the full available width: roughly x10–326 within a 330-wide panel (96%), from y138 to y430. Its outer structure is a physical chrome entrance with multiple recessed light rails. It must not read as a thin stroked circle.
- The top of the housing tapers into sharp angled metal shoulders; the lower half has a substantial curved lower lip and grounded base. Thickness and reflections vary by sector. This makes the entry read as perspective architecture rather than a constant-width symmetric hoop.
- Approximate bright channel width at source scale is 10–15 pixels distributed across multiple rails, with narrow white-hot cores inside saturated blue/green/red, dark grooves and steel beside it. Broad saturated glow is visible in the nearby corridor and floor, while the logo remains legible.
- Venue fills the ring interior, with luminous stadium lights across the sides and the playing surface visible below the emblem. Logo is large but leaves enough venue visible to establish depth.
- The floor is reflective and coloured. Keep it compact: a large empty floor band separates ring from CTA in older screenshots and weakens the dense target.
